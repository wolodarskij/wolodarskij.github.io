## Loop

Hunt for supplies, host midnight auctions, decide which truth to sell. The city remembers. Prices shift. Allies vanish.

## Systems

- RL agents set market demand and punish selfish streaks.
- AI rumor engine fabricates side quests when your reputation dips below zero.
- Minimal UI, maximal dread: everything important is hidden in overheard chatter.

## Roadmap

More systemic sabotage, polished controller support, and a public WebGL slice.
