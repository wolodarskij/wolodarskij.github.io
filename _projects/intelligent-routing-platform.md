## Challenge

Growing parcel volume across 14 countries strained the legacy routing system. Manual overrides were common and costly.

## Approach

- Reframed the business problem around customer promises and delivery economics.
- Introduced experimentation infrastructure to evaluate new routing strategies safely.
- Migrated to a modular platform that ingests signals from demand forecasts, courier networks, and traffic providers.

## Result

The routing platform adapts in real time while giving operations teams transparency and control. The company unlocked new premium services and expanded into two additional markets.
