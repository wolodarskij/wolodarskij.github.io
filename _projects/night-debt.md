## Core idea

A courtroom brawl set inside a dying city. Every hearing is a run. Burn your credibility for power, or stay clean and watch witnesses disappear.

## Current solve

- Local language model generates jury bias in real time, pushing back when you repeat tactics.
- Evidence deckbuilding forces a choice between safe testimonies and cursed items that bend the rules.
- Failures persist: past verdicts unlock new shortcuts but stigmatize future allies.

## Next steps

Tuning the shame economy so victories taste dirty. Controllers next.
