## Why it exists

It started as a 48-hour jam about radio ghosts. I kept the tension but swapped cheap jump scares for trust-testing communication.

## How it works

- AI medium suggests translations based on how players describe the static, then lies if you get lazy.
- Rooms reset with altered signals when players speak over each other.
- Every solved broadcast opens a memory vault; open too many and the house rewrites itself.

## In progress

Network play. Spatial audio upgrades. A promise that silence is always suspicious.
