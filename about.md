## Hi, I’m Alexandr — an engineer obsessed with momentum

I spent the last decade helping ambitious product teams design, build, and operate software that grows with their business. From IoT analytics platforms to consumer marketplaces, the through line has always been **shipping quickly without sacrificing reliability**.

### Philosophy

- **Customer-first discovery.** You cannot fix what you do not understand. I push teams to ground every roadmap conversation in the user journey and measurable outcomes.
- **Flow, not heroics.** Sustainable delivery beats burnout. I focus on automation, observability, and developer ergonomics so teams can ship confidently every day.
- **Calm operations.** Incident-ready architectures, sane on-call rotations, and clear playbooks keep product velocity high even as complexity grows.

### Recent focus areas

- Guiding a marketplace rebuild from a monolithic Rails codebase to a modular Next.js & NestJS platform delivering 3× faster feature throughput.
- Designing platform guardrails that cut mean-time-to-recovery below 15 minutes for a fintech processing millions of transactions weekly.
- Coaching product leads on dual-track discovery so experiments roll out within the same sprint.

### Outside of work

You can usually find me sketching service diagrams on café napkins, mentoring early-stage CTOs, or adding the finishing touches to a synthwave playlist. I split my time between Berlin and Vilnius and work comfortably across EU and US timezones.
